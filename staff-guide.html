1. System Access & Login

Amcal Woy Woy Roster is the official roster and timeclocking platform for Amcal Pharmacy Woy Woy.

1.1 App Web Address & PWA Installation

  • Official App URL: Access anytime from browser at https://woywoyamcalroster.vercel.app
  • iPhone (iOS) Home Screen App: Open Safari -> Tap Share -> Tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android App: Open Chrome -> Tap menu dots -> Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".

1.2 Employee Account Registration & Password Reset

  • Invitation Link: New staff receive a link (?invite=CODE) generated by the Pharmacist Manager to register.
  • Login: Enter your registered email address and password.
  • Forgot Password: Click "Forgot Password?" to receive an instant recovery link via email.
Offline PWA Support: If mobile data drops while clocking in/out, timecard stamps queue safely on your device and sync automatically when internet restores.

2. Employee Dashboard Overview

  • My Weekly Summary: Displays your total scheduled hours for the week alongside your estimated earnings.
  • My Timeclock Status: Shows whether you are currently Not Clocked In, Working (Clocked In), or On Break.
  • My Next Shift: Displays your upcoming shift date, start time, end time, and assigned role.

3. Viewing Schedules & Daily Timeline

  • Weekly Scheduler Grid: View 7-day rosters from Monday to Sunday with color-coded roles (Pharmacist, Dispensary Tech, Assistant, Webster).
  • Daily View & Timeline: Interactive coverage timeline graph (8:30 AM – 5:30 PM) plus daily shift checklist table.
  • Roster Print: Click "Print Roster" for A4 Landscape noticeboard printing.

4. Pharmacy Industry Award 2026 Break Rules

All shifts strictly follow the latest Pharmacy Industry Award 2026 [MA000084] (Clause 16):

Shift Duration 🍱 Unpaid Meal Break (Lunch) ☕ Paid Rest Break (10 Min)
Less than 4 Hours No Break Required No Break Required
4.0 Hours to < 5.0 Hours No Break Required 1 x 10-Min Paid Rest
5.0 Hours to < 7.6 Hours 1 x 30-Min Unpaid Lunch 1 x 10-Min Paid Rest
7.6 Hours or More (Full Shift) 1 x 30-Min Unpaid Lunch 2 x 10-Min Paid Rest (Morning & Afternoon)
Paid Rest Breaks Protection: 10-minute rest breaks are PAID by law and are NEVER deducted from your paid timecard hours. Only the 30-minute Lunch Break is unpaid.

5. Time Clock Terminal & GPS Geofencing

  • Store GPS Geofencing: Clocking in requires location access within ~500m of Amcal Pharmacy Woy Woy (-33.4842, 151.3259).
  • Clock In / Clock Out: Select your name, tap "Clock In" at start of shift, and "Clock Out" when finished.
  • Break Tracking Buttons:
    • 🍱 30m Lunch (Unpaid): Start 30-minute unpaid lunch break
    • ☕ 10m Rest (Paid): Start 10-minute paid tea/rest break
    • 🟢 End Break: Return to active working status

6. Shift Swaps & Time Off Requests

  • Shift Swap Board: Initiate peer shift swap requests. Once accepted by your colleague and approved by the Manager, the roster updates automatically.
  • Requesting Leave: Submit Annual Leave, Sick Leave, or Carer's Leave under the Time Off tab and track approval status in real time.
